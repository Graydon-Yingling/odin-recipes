Tacos

How to Make

This dish is an Mexican classic loved all over and takes just minutes to prepare.

Ingredients

  • Beef or chicken
  • Mexican blend cheese
  • Sour cream
  • Chopped tomatos
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Taco shells

Steps

  1. Cook your meat in a medium heat pan.
  2. Warm tortillas or taco shells to your liking.
  3. Put meat into shell and add desired toppings. Serve.

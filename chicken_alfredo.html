Chicken Alfredo

How to Make

This dish is an Italian pasta dish that features noodles smothered in a cheesy, creamy sauce and topped with chicken.

Ingredients

  • Chickem
  • Parmesean cheese
  • Cream
  • Garlic
  • Seasoning
  • Pasta

Steps

  1. Season and put chicken into a medium-heat pan. Cook for 5 minutes each side.
  2. Boil a large pot of water, then add pasta. Cook till just underdone.
  3. In a sauce pan, combine cheese, minced garlic, and cream and whisk until smooth. Add seasoning.
  4. Once pasta is reeady, add it to the sauce pan and coat noodles in sauce.
  5. Plate creamy pasta and top with cut-up chicken. Serve.

Grilled Cheese

How to Make

This dish is an simple classic that goes well with tomato soup or just by itself!.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Spread one side of each slice of bread with butter.
  2. Add cheese between the unbuttered side of bread slices.
  3. Cook in medium-low pan until each side is golden brown. Serve
